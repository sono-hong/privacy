Go Poop does not collect your data. Everything you record stays on your own devices and in your own iCloud account. We cannot see it, and we never send it anywhere else. There is no analytics, no advertising, and no tracking in this app.

This policy explains that in full, and tells you exactly what happens to the information you enter. It applies to the Go Poop app for iPhone and Apple Watch, including its Apple Watch complication.

Who we are

Go Poop is published by {{ site.publisher }}, an independent developer. You can reach us at {{ site.contact_email }}.

What information the app handles

Go Poop is a bowel movement tracker. When you use it, you may create records containing:

  • The date and time of a bowel movement
  • A stool consistency rating on the Bristol Stool Scale (1–7), if you choose to enter one
  • An optional short note that you type yourself
  • Which device the record was made on (iPhone, Apple Watch, or a notification action)

You may also change settings such as your reminder threshold and reminder time.

That is the complete list. The app does not ask for your name, email address, phone number, date of birth, location, contacts, photos, or any other personal information.

Where that information is stored

On your device

Your records are stored in a database on your iPhone and Apple Watch. This database is inside the app's private storage area, which other apps cannot read.

In your iCloud account

So that your iPhone and Apple Watch show the same records, Go Poop syncs them through Apple's CloudKit private database — your own personal iCloud storage.

This is important, so we want to be precise about it:

  • The data is stored in your iCloud account, not on our servers. We do not operate any server for this app.
  • A CloudKit private database is only accessible to the iCloud account that owns it. We cannot read it. Not through the app, not through Apple's developer tools, not by any other means available to us.
  • If you are not signed in to iCloud, the app simply stores everything locally on the device and does not sync. It keeps working.
  • Your use of iCloud is governed by Apple's Privacy Policy.

Shared with the Apple Watch complication

If you add the Go Poop complication to your watch face, the app writes a small amount of information to a shared storage area on your own device so the complication can display it: the date of your most recent record, the date you first launched the app, and your reminder threshold setting.

The contents of your records — the stool ratings and your notes — are never written there. The complication only needs to show a number of days.

Notifications

If you turn on reminders and grant notification permission, Go Poop schedules local notifications on your device. These are created and delivered entirely by your iPhone. Nothing is sent to us or to a push server we control, and the content of your records is not included in them.

The app also uses Apple's silent push mechanism, which is what allows CloudKit to tell your devices that something changed on another device. These silent messages carry no content of yours and produce no visible alert.

You can turn reminders off at any time in the app's Settings screen, or revoke notification permission entirely in iOS Settings.

How to delete your data

You are in control of everything the app holds.

  • Delete a single record — open it in the app and choose Delete.
  • Delete everything — Settings › Delete all data. This removes your records from the device and from your iCloud, on every device signed in to the same account.
  • Delete the app — removing Go Poop deletes the local database. To remove the iCloud copy as well, use Delete all data before uninstalling, or delete the app's data from Settings › [your name] › iCloud › Manage Account Storage.

Because we never hold your data, there is nothing for you to request from us and nothing for us to delete on your behalf. Deletion is immediate and entirely in your hands.
