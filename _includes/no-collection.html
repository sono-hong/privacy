What we do not do

  • We do not collect your data. Nothing you enter is transmitted to us.
  • We do not use analytics. There is no Google Analytics, Firebase, Crashlytics, Mixpanel, Amplitude, Sentry, or any comparable service in this app.
  • We do not show advertising and do not include any advertising SDK.
  • We do not track you across apps or websites, and we do not access the Advertising Identifier (IDFA).
  • We do not sell, rent, or share your information with anyone. We have nothing to sell.
  • We do not create a user account. There is no sign-up and no password.
  • We do not read your device identifiers, serial number, or phone number.

The app contains no networking code of its own. Its only network activity is the iCloud synchronisation performed by the operating system on your behalf.
