Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have rights under laws such as the GDPR (Europe), the UK GDPR, or the CCPA/CPRA (California) — to access, correct, export, or delete personal data a company holds about you, and to object to its sale.

Those rights are satisfied here in an unusual way: we hold no personal data about you at all. There is no profile, no identifier, and no record on our side to access or erase. Your data is on your devices and in your own iCloud account, where you can read, edit, and delete it directly at any time.
