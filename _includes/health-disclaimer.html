Health information

The records this app keeps are personal health information, and we treat them accordingly: they stay on your device and in your private iCloud, and we cannot see them. The app does not read from or write to Apple Health, and does not access any other health data on your device.

{{ page.app }} is not a medical device. It is a self-tracking tool. It does not diagnose, treat, cure, or prevent any condition, and nothing it displays is medical advice. If you are worried about your health, please talk to a doctor.
