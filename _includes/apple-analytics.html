Information Apple may provide to us

We want to be complete rather than flattering, so this section matters.

If you have chosen to share analytics with app developers in your device settings (Settings › Privacy & Security › Analytics & Improvements), Apple may give us aggregated and anonymised reports about the app — for example how many devices launched it, or an anonymised crash report. This is provided by Apple, not by the app, and it never contains your records or anything that identifies you. You can turn it off in that same settings screen.

If this app is a paid app or offers purchases, Apple handles the entire transaction. Apple provides us with sales figures. We never receive your payment details — no card number, no billing address, no Apple ID.
