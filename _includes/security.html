Security

Data stored on your device is protected by iOS and watchOS file protection, which is tied to your device passcode. Data synced through iCloud is encrypted in transit and at rest by Apple. Keeping a passcode on your devices, and keeping your Apple Account secure, is the most effective thing you can do to protect your records.

No system is perfect, and we will not claim otherwise. What we can say is that we have deliberately built the app so that there is no server of ours to breach and no copy of your data for us to lose.
