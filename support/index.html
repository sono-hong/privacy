Common questions

My iPhone and Apple Watch show different records

Records sync through your own iCloud account, so both devices must be signed in to the same Apple Account, and both need a network connection. Syncing is not instant — it usually takes a few seconds to a few minutes.

If something is still missing, close the app and open it again. Go Poop reloads whenever it comes back to the foreground.

The Apple Watch app is missing

Open the Watch app on your iPhone, scroll down to Available Apps, and install Go Poop from there. Delivery can take a few minutes.

I am not signed in to iCloud

Go Poop still works. Everything is stored on the device instead, and nothing syncs until you sign in.

How do I delete my data?

Settings › Delete all data. This erases your records from every device signed in to the same iCloud account. It cannot be undone.

We never hold a copy, so there is nothing for us to delete on your behalf.

How do I change the language?

Settings › Language. You can pick English or Japanese, or let the app follow your device settings. The choice syncs to your Apple Watch.

Reminders are not arriving

Check that notifications are allowed in iOS Settings › Go Poop, and that reminders are switched on in the app. The app's Settings screen lists the next scheduled reminders so you can see exactly what will fire and when.

Privacy

Go Poop collects nothing. See the privacy policy for the details.

A note about health

Go Poop is not a medical device. It is a self-tracking tool and does not diagnose, treat, or prevent any condition. Talk to a doctor about anything that worries you.
